Coruscant Headquarters, 11 March 2024

Empire Incorporated has selected Kuat Drive Yards to design and deliver hull plating sections and gantry crane assemblies for Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard. Work starts in March 2024.

"Kuat Drive Yards brings decades of orbital fabrication experience," said Rennick Solberg, Director, Procurement & Supply Chain.

> About Empire Incorporated: Empire Incorporated designs and builds orbital habitats, shipyards and research facilities. Its flagship program, Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard, will house resident crews and build and refit civilian ships. The company is based at Coruscant Headquarters. Media enquiries: press@empire-inc.example.
