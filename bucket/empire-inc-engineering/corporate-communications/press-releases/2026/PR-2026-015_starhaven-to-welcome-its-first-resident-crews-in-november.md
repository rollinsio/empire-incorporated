Coruscant Headquarters, 25 August 2026

The first resident crews are expected to move into Starhaven's habitation blocks in November 2026. Schools, the medical centre and the first mess halls will open at the same time.

> About Empire Incorporated: Empire Incorporated designs and builds orbital habitats, shipyards and research facilities. Its flagship program, Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard, will house resident crews and build and refit civilian ships. The company is based at Coruscant Headquarters. Media enquiries: press@empire-inc.example.
