Coruscant Headquarters, 20 January 2026

Mira Kestrel will head the Reactor & Power Systems department from 1 February 2026. Kestrel succeeds Aldric Voss, who moves to the Program Management Office as Senior Advisor.

Kestrel joined the company in 2015 as a thermal engineer and has been Deputy Director of the department since 2022.

> About Empire Incorporated: Empire Incorporated designs and builds orbital habitats, shipyards and research facilities. Its flagship program, Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard, will house resident crews and build and refit civilian ships. The company is based at Coruscant Headquarters.
