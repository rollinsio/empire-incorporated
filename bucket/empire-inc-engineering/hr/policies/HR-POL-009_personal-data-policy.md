This policy applies to all employees of Empire Incorporated unless a site policy says otherwise.

## 1. Scope

Personal data covers salaries, performance reviews, home addresses, personal comlink numbers and emergency contacts.

## 2. Labelling and access

Documents containing personal data are labelled Restricted. Only Personnel Administration and the employee's line management may open them.

## 3. Retention

Personnel files are kept for 6 years after employment ends and then deleted.

Effective 2025-01-01. Policy owner: Pell Amaranth, Director, Personnel Administration.
