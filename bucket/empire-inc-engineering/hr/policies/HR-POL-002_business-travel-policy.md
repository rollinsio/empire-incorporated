This policy applies to all employees of Empire Incorporated unless a site policy says otherwise.

## 1. Booking

All business travel is booked through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure.

## 2. Class of travel

Economy class is used for journeys under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for journeys of 6 hours or more, including hyperspace transfers.

## 3. Daily allowance

The daily allowance (per diem) for meals and incidentals is 85 credits per travel day.

Effective 2024-07-01. Policy owner: Odessa Varga, Chief Financial Officer.
