This policy applies to all employees of Empire Incorporated unless a site policy says otherwise.

## 1. Claims

Expense claims are submitted within 30 days of the expense, with receipts for every item above 25 credits.

## 2. Approval and payment

The line manager approves the claim; Finance reimburses approved claims within 10 working days.

Effective 2024-07-01. Policy owner: Odessa Varga, Chief Financial Officer.
