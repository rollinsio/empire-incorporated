This policy applies to all employees of Empire Incorporated unless a site policy says otherwise.

## 1. Labels

Empire Incorporated uses four sensitivity labels. Every document carries exactly one label in its header and footer.

| Label | German label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Public | ÖFFENTLICH | Approved for release outside the company. |
| Internal | INTERN | Any employee may see it; not for release outside the company. |
| Confidential | VERTRAULICH | Business-sensitive: budgets, contract prices, supplier terms and cost data. Employees with a business need. |
| Restricted | STRENG VERTRAULICH | Personal data: salaries, performance reviews and home contact details. Personnel staff and line management only. |

## 2. Handling

When in doubt, choose the higher label and ask the document owner. Documents that carry the wrong label are reported to the document owner for relabelling.

Effective 2024-01-01. Policy owner: Corvin Ashgrove, General Counsel.
