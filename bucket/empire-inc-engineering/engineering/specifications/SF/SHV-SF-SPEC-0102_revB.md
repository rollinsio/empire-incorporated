## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the performance, interface and verification requirements for the structural rib (SF-RIB) of the Superstructure & Frame on Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard.

## 2. Scope

Fabrication and installation are performed under work package WP-SHV-SF-03 (WBS 1.14.3), owned by Vela Wade.

The prime supplier for flight hardware is Chandrila Atmospherics. Responsible engineer: Ulla Hawthorne.

## 4. Description

The superstructure is the station's load-bearing frame: structural ribs, hull plating sections, the equatorial service ring and the antenna masts.

The structural rib is delivered as a set of line-replaceable assemblies, integrated on station by Construction Operations and verified by Quality Assurance & Test.

## 5. Performance requirements

| ID | Requirement | Value | Tolerance | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SF-RIB-R01 | The structural rib shall provide a rib count of 612 ea. | 612 ea | ±2% | Demonstration |
| SF-RIB-R02 | The structural rib shall provide a rib depth of 127 m. | 127 m | ±1% | Inspection |

### 5.1 General requirements

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| SF-RIB-G01 | Maintainability: Line-replaceable units shall be replaceable by a crew of four within one shift. | Inspection |
| SF-RIB-G02 | Safety: Single faults shall not cause loss of the zone or injury to crew. | Analysis |
| SF-RIB-G03 | Workmanship: Welds and fasteners shall meet the station workmanship standard SHV-QA-STD-0002. | Inspection |
| SF-RIB-G04 | Marking: Every assembly shall carry its drawing number, revision and serial number. | Analysis |
| SF-RIB-G05 | Documentation: Acceptance data packages shall be delivered with every unit. | Inspection |
