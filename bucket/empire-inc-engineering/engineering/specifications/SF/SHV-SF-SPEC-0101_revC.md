## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the performance, interface and verification requirements for the hull plating section (SF-HPS) of the Superstructure & Frame on Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard.

## 2. Scope

Fabrication and installation are performed under work package WP-SHV-SF-02 (WBS 1.14.2), owned by Carys Dravis.

The prime supplier for flight hardware is Kuat Drive Yards. Responsible engineer: Jarek Keller.

## 4. Description

The superstructure is the station's load-bearing frame: structural ribs, hull plating sections, the equatorial service ring and the antenna masts.

The hull plating section is delivered as a set of line-replaceable assemblies, integrated on station by Construction Operations and verified by Quality Assurance & Test.

## 5. Performance requirements

| ID | Requirement | Value | Tolerance | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SF-HPS-R01 | The hull plating section shall provide a plate thickness of 6.3 m. | 6.3 m | ±5% | Demonstration |
| SF-HPS-R02 | The hull plating section shall provide a section count of 20,889 ea. | 20,889 ea | ±5% | Inspection |

### 5.1 General requirements

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| SF-HPS-G01 | Maintainability: Line-replaceable units shall be replaceable by a crew of four within one shift. | Inspection |
| SF-HPS-G02 | Safety: Single faults shall not cause loss of the zone or injury to crew. | Analysis |
| SF-HPS-G03 | Workmanship: Welds and fasteners shall meet the station workmanship standard SHV-QA-STD-0002. | Inspection |
| SF-HPS-G04 | Marking: Every assembly shall carry its drawing number, revision and serial number. | Analysis |
| SF-HPS-G05 | Documentation: Acceptance data packages shall be delivered with every unit. | Inspection |

Each requirement is verified by the method stated in section 5, following SHV-QA-PLN-0004. Acceptance test reports are filed under the SF test-report series.
