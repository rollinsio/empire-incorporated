## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the performance, interface and verification requirements for the recreation deck (HA-REC) of the Habitation Blocks on Starhaven Orbital Habitat & Shipyard.

## 2. Scope

Fabrication and installation are performed under work package WP-SHV-HA-06 (WBS 1.9.6), owned by Sola Quarrel.

The prime supplier for flight hardware is Abregado Droid Works. Responsible engineer: Bale Pike.

## 4. Description

The habitation blocks house the resident crew and their families: quarters, mess halls, the medical centre, schools and recreation decks.

The recreation deck is delivered as a set of line-replaceable assemblies, integrated on station by Construction Operations and verified by Quality Assurance & Test.

## 5. Performance requirements

| ID | Requirement | Value | Tolerance | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HA-REC-R01 | The recreation deck shall provide a deck area of 15,705 m². | 15,705 m² | ±2% | Demonstration |

### 5.1 General requirements

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| HA-REC-G01 | Maintainability: Line-replaceable units shall be replaceable by a crew of four within one shift. | Inspection |
| HA-REC-G02 | Safety: Single faults shall not cause loss of the zone or injury to crew. | Analysis |
| HA-REC-G03 | Workmanship: Welds and fasteners shall meet the station workmanship standard SHV-QA-STD-0002. | Inspection |
| HA-REC-G04 | Marking: Every assembly shall carry its drawing number, revision and serial number. | Analysis |
| HA-REC-G05 | Documentation: Acceptance data packages shall be delivered with every unit. | Inspection |

## 6. Environmental conditions

| Condition | Value |
|---|---|
| Operating pressure | Vacuum to 110 kPa |
| Radiation, total dose | Station design environment, class R3 |
| Thermal cycling | 200 cycles, operating range |
| Vibration | Station-keeping manoeuvre profile, level 2 |
| Electromagnetic compatibility | Station EMC standard, category B |

## 7. Verification

Each requirement is verified by the method stated in section 5, following SHV-QA-PLN-0004. Acceptance test reports are filed under the HA test-report series.
