## Working time

The standard week is 38 hours in two shifts. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate.

## Annual leave

Every employee receives 27 days of annual leave, plus the Kuat founding day.

## Safety

Hard hats, harnesses and vacuum suits are mandatory in the fabrication bays.
