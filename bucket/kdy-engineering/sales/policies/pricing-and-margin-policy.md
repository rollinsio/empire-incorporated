## 1. Target margins

| Customer tier | Target margin |
|---|---|
| Key accounts | 22.0% |
| Standard customers | 28.0% |

No quote may go out below a 15.0% margin.

## 2. Approvals

- Quotes at or above the tier target: Head of Sales & Key Accounts.
- Quotes below the tier target: Chief Financial Officer.
- Every key-account quote records a walk-away price before negotiation starts.
