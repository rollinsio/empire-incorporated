# Kuat Drive Yards to supply hull plating and gantry cranes for Starhaven

|---|---|

Kuat Drive Yards has signed a multi-year agreement with Empire Incorporated to supply hull plating sections, gantry crane hoists, slipway rails and dry dock seals for the Starhaven orbital habitat and shipyard.

Deliveries run from 2024 to 2027 from the Kuat fabrication bays. Merel Danvik leads the account.
